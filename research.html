Research Topics

Medical Microbiology

Study microorganisms such as bacteria, viruses and fungi that can cause disease.

Clinical Chemistry

Study chemical substances in blood and other body fluids to help diagnose diseases.

Hematology

Study blood, blood cells and disorders affecting the blood and blood-forming organs.

Immunology

Study the immune system and how the body responds to infections and diseases.

Parasitology

Study parasites and the diseases they can cause in humans.

Why Research Matters

Laboratory research helps scientists understand diseases, improve diagnostic methods and contribute to better healthcare.
